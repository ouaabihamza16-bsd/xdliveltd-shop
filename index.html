About us

The work behind the performance

XD LIVE LTD is a London-based company that provides support services to the performing arts. We look after the practical side of live performances and events, from planning and scheduling through to delivery on the day.

The company was incorporated in England and Wales on 18 January 2022 under company number 13855845. Its registered office is in St John's Wood, London NW8, and our work is focused on London.

We're a small, director-led company. That keeps things straightforward: you talk directly to the person responsible for the work, and plans don't get lost between layers of management.

Services

What we do

Support for live performance covers a lot of moving parts. These are the areas we help with.

Production support

Planning and coordinating the practical side of a live show or event. We help put together schedules and running orders, keep track of who needs to be where and when, and see the plan through on the day.

Technical and stage support

Help with the staging, sound and lighting side of a performance. We work alongside venues and their technical suppliers to coordinate requirements, set-up times and changeovers, so the stage is ready when the performers are.

Artist and show coordination

A clear line of communication between performers, promoters and venues. We pass on requirements, confirm arrangements and keep everyone up to date, so the performance runs smoothly from arrival to finish.

Event delivery

Support for one-off concerts, showcases and private or corporate performances in London. We help bring the different pieces together and stay on hand to deal with the details as the event happens.
